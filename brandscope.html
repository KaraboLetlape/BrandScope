OUR STORY

ABOUT BRANDSCOPE

BrandScope was established in 2025 by two friends with a shared passion for clothing, thrifting and streetwear.

After spending time thrifting and understanding the market, we recognized an opportunity to provide quality branded clothing to people who share the same appreciation for fashion and unique pieces.

BrandScope is a South African clothing resale platform based in Johannesburg. We carefully source and select branded clothing and accessories, including T-shirts, hoodies, sweaters, jackets, pants, caps and beanies.

Our goal is simple: provide customers with quality branded streetwear while making the buying experience simple, accessible and reliable.
